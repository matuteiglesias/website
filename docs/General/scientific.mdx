# Research and computational science

The current research entrypoint is [Research](/research), organized around aggregation, probabilistic specialization
indices, and geographic correlations. The [full research programme](https://thesis.matuteiglesias.link/docs/aggregation)
includes the doctoral thesis, selected publications, working papers and current questions.

Research maturity varies by output: the 2021 location-quotient article is peer-reviewed; _Concentration Is Not Scaling_
is a current manuscript; geographic correlations remain a working-paper line with unresolved extensions.
