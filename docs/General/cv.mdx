# Professional summary

Versatile AI engineer and data scientist with over eight years of experience building production ML pipelines, data
engineering systems, and AI-enabled automation for public sector and research settings. Works at the intersection of
engineering and policy: designs reliable data pipelines, materializes reproducible experiments, and translates results
into operational tools for decision makers. Technical strengths include Python, SQL, production APIs, vector search and
IA workflow deployment, and automation of CI/CD workflows.

# Skills

**Programming and infra**: Python, R, SQL, FastAPI, Docker, GitHub Actions, CI/CD **LLMs and retrieval**: LangChain,
PromptFlow, ChromaDB, vector search, RAG architectures **Data engineering**: ETL design, orchestration, data quality,
semantic indexing **ML and research**: supervised models, spatial models, experiment logging, reproducibility **Cloud**:
GCP, AWS, Azure (deployment and managed services) **Visualization and tools**: pandas, matplotlib, Streamlit, Jupyter
**Project practice**: Agile workflows, runbooks, stakeholder communication
