Welcome! I’m **Matías Iglesias**, a **Scientific Data & AI Engineer** building reliable systems for data, documents,
models, and automation.

My work combines a scientific background in **physics and economics** with hands-on engineering in **Python, SQL,
machine learning, GenAI, retrieval systems, data pipelines, and reproducible analytical infrastructure**.

I focus on turning complex and messy information into systems that can be queried, evaluated, automated, and used for
decisions: from research-grade socioeconomic measurement to AI-powered knowledge infrastructure and operational data
products.

---

## What I Build

- **AI & Data Systems**: pipelines, RAG systems, LLM workflows, retrieval layers, automation, and data products.
- **Scientific & Quantitative Work**: statistical modeling, computational economics, poverty measurement, and
  reproducible research.
- **Civic & Institutional Systems**: public data infrastructure, policy-facing analytics, governance trackers, and
  institutional memory tools.
- **Selected Projects**: concrete deployments, apps, research tools, and automation systems.
- **CV**: background, experience, technical skills, and contact.
