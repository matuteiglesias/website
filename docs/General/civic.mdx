## 📊 Socioeconomic Measurement and Research Infrastructure

1. **I developed Survey-Census harmonization engines**, which integrates national microdata (EPH, 2022 Census, Redatam)
   to produce consistent poverty and income indicators with transparent reproducibility.

2. **I built the Poverty Atlas**, a live geospatial platform that translates survey and census data into public poverty
   maps used for social policy analysis.

3. **I was responsible for data engineering for research teams in economics and public policy**, aligning datasets for
   development economics projects.

4. **I expanded the boundaries of micro-macro traceability** in my doctoral research on how micro shocks aggregate into
   macro volatility using computational and nonlinear systems methods that reveal serious problems in established
   economics papers.
