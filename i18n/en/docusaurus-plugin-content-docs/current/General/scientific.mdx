# Research and computational science

## Book (2026)

**Matías Iglesias, _Mathematics and Economics of Aggregation_ (2026).** Eight chapters connecting weights, concentration, covariance, nonlinear transformations, network propagation, population scaling and responsible interpretation.

[Explore the book](/books/mathematics-and-economics-of-aggregation)

The current research entrypoint is [Research](/research), organized around aggregation, probabilistic specialization indices, and geographic correlations. The [full research programme](https://thesis.matuteiglesias.link/docs/aggregation) includes the doctoral thesis, selected publications, working papers and current questions.

Research maturity varies by output: the 2021 location-quotient article is peer-reviewed; _Concentration Is Not Scaling_ is a current manuscript; geographic correlations remain a working-paper line with unresolved extensions.
